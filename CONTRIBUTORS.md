# Contributors

Authors: Formal Frontier Agents. The original project development and later
contributions were AI-assisted. The roles below distinguish original proofs,
subsequent adaptations, reader work and review; collective author credit is
not a claim that one contributor wrote every result or owns copyright.

## Mathematical development

- **Atlas** developed the original projective-space mathematics later assembled
  into this library, including basic opens, the positive-degree radical
  criterion, homogeneous-coordinate points and dimensions, polynomial charts,
  geometry, quotient Proj, graded transport and symmetric-algebra models.
  Atlas selected and accepted distinct contributors' later work as the
  responsible maintainer; selection is not authorship of their new proofs.
- **Later Formal Frontier AI-agent contributors** adapted Atlas's original
  mathematical files, including polynomial charts and
  localizations, geometric properties, quotient and symmetric-algebra results.
  Other agents built the public aggregate import and direct-import clients.
- **The geometric-cover contributor** developed `GlobalSections`, its direct
  client and starting guide; a separate contributor adapted it
  in this library. The chart and gluing proof expression adapts Andrew Yang's
  2024 mathlib work, with his original notice retained in the producer.
- **The unit-scaling contributor** authored the whole-arrow comparison and its
  direct-import client; a separate contributor adapted the producer for this
  library and wrote the guide. Its chart construction and gluing adapt Andrew
  Yang's work.
- **The degree-scaled-map contributor** authored the positive-multiplier,
  natural-open-domain Proj morphism and its client. Other contributors prepared
  the Graded Rings degree-multiplying localization and adapted this library's
  morphism to it. The former is imported, not copied here. Mathlib
  projective-chart and homogeneous-localization authors retain their notices.
- **A distinct point-prime contributor** proved ordinary-prime contraction for
  every ring element on that open domain and wrote its direct client. Another
  contributor adapted the point leaf and guide for this library; the earlier
  degree-scaled producer is its mathematical input, not its author.
- **A positive-Veronese contributor** developed the selected-component algebra,
  chart equivalences and whole-scheme isomorphism. Graded Rings supplies the
  algebra; another contributor adapted the geometric development and
  wrote this library's seven-example client and guide. Ring-only examples
  remain with Graded Rings.
- **The coherent-tail algebra contributor** developed the original high-tail
  equivalence; a separate contributor prepared its algebraic implementation for
  Graded Rings. **A different geometry contributor** developed the original
  whole-scheme Proj isomorphism and coefficient triangles. Another contributor
  adapted that geometry to the graded-ring dependencies, wrote the client and
  guide and included it in the public import.
- **A distinct Formal Frontier AI-agent contributor** proved the
  projective-spectrum instance and its finite-coordinate polynomial corollary,
  and wrote a direct client. The proofs use Mathlib's degree-zero Noetherian
  and scheme APIs and the Graded Rings finite-type bridge.

## Reader and review work

Formal Frontier AI-agent contributors wrote the three original private reader
example modules, the module/API map and reader guide. A separate contributor
selected and corrected their presentation and headers. Other contributors
prepared source assemblies and updated documentation, metadata and notices
without thereby authoring the proofs they assembled. Independent, author-distinct
reviewers assessed the substantive contributions at their respective revisions;
Atlas made the separate maintainer acceptance and integration decisions. The
source-specific correspondence and coverage decisions remain outside this
reusable library.
