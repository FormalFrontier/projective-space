# Veronese charts and the whole projective scheme

Import `ProjectiveSpace.Veronese` for this API or `ProjectiveSpace` for the
aggregate library. The seven geometric checks in
[`VeroneseClient.lean`](../ProjectiveSpaceTest/VeroneseClient.lean) use the
direct public leaf import. The selected-ring construction and degree-zero
interfaces are provided by `GradedRings.Veronese`; see its
[standalone ring guide](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/graded-rings/blob/0a5c69da9b24beb5c33ddddbfe4d0067e53146ec/docs/Veronese.md).

## Construction and assumptions

Let `S` be a commutative ring and `𝒮 : ℕ → σ` a grading with
`[SetLike σ S] [AddSubgroupClass σ S] [GradedRing 𝒮]`. For **any** `n : ℕ`,
`GradedRing.Veronese.VeroneseRing 𝒮 n` is the direct sum of the **whole**
old components `𝒮 (n * j)`, with `component 𝒮 n j` its new degree-`j`
component. It is neither a regrading of every element of `S` nor the
subalgebra generated by the one component `𝒮 n`.

The canonical `inclusion 𝒮 n : VeroneseRing 𝒮 n →+* S` describes these
selected components at every `n`. **Injectivity of the inclusion, the
degree-zero `zeroRingEquiv`, the chart equivalences and the global `Proj`
isomorphism all require `hn : 0 < n`.** The unqualified ring construction
does not imply invariance of `Proj` at `n = 0`. Neither finite generation,
generation in degree one, a domain, reducedness, a field, nontriviality nor
regularity of chart denominators is assumed for this API.

## Charts and gluing

In `AlgebraicGeometry.Proj.Veronese`, for `f : VeroneseRing 𝒮 n` in
`component 𝒮 n j`, `chartHom 𝒮 n f` is the degree-scaled ring map
between the corresponding `Away` localizations. Its
`chartHom_injective` proof uses inclusion injectivity through ordinary
localization, not cancellation by `f`; `chartHom_surjective` lifts every
homogeneous numerator to the reindexed component. Thus
`chartEquiv 𝒮 n hn j f hf` identifies **entire** degree-zero localizations
even for `f = 0`, nilpotent `f`, zero divisors, and the zero ring.
`chartEquiv_mk` computes the map on fractions, while
`chartHom_awayMap` and `chartEquiv_awayMap` commute with restrictions to
product charts.

The positive-index `domain_eq_top` identifies the natural domain of
`degreeScaledMap` with all of `Proj 𝒮` using powers of old positive forms;
it does **not** assert equality of irrelevant ideals or of their radicals.
`domainIso` converts this top open into the whole source scheme, and
`forward 𝒮 n hn : Proj 𝒮 ⟶ Proj (component 𝒮 n)` is precisely the
published degree-scaled arrow on that whole domain. The target is covered
by positive basic opens (`iSup_basicOpen_eq_top`), and
`forward_restrict_chart` identifies every restricted arrow with the
spectrum map of `chartHom`. Target-local gluing of these chart
equivalences gives `isIso_forward` and the **whole sheaf-bearing scheme
isomorphism** `schemeIso 𝒮 n hn : Proj 𝒮 ≅ Proj (component 𝒮 n)`.
`schemeIso_hom` states that its forward arrow is exactly `forward`.

## Points and coefficient rings

For any `p : Proj 𝒮`, `forward_pointIdeal` identifies the whole ordinary
prime ideal of `forward 𝒮 n hn p` with
`Ideal.comap (inclusion 𝒮 n) (p.asHomogeneousIdeal.toIdeal)`;
`forward_mem_pointIdeal` specializes to **any** element of the selected
ring, including degree-zero and inhomogeneous elements.
`forward_preimage_basicOpen` describes the preimage of each positive-degree
target basic open. `degreeZeroRingHom_eq` identifies the canonical
degree-zero map with `zeroRingEquiv`, and `forward_toSpecZero` proves the
whole-arrow triangle with `toSpecZero` and this coefficient-ring equivalence;
no auxiliary base algebra is required.

Vakil, *The Rising Sea* (October 21, 2025), §7.4.4, Exercise 7.4.D,
printed p. 215, is mathematical inspiration. The source's finite-generation
context is **not** an extra Lean hypothesis here. This API does not provide
finite-generation results or further Veronese-generation/embedding claims.

## Using the module

The [production module](../ProjectiveSpace/Veronese.lean) exports the chart,
point and whole-scheme results; the [direct client](../ProjectiveSpaceTest/VeroneseClient.lean)
checks seven geometric uses. Graded Rings supplies the selected-component
algebra through the pinned official dependency. The [reader guide](../README.md)
gives the cache-first four-target build recipe.
